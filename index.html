    Settings and how this works

    The public Solana endpoint is rate-limited. A free RPC from any provider is faster; it is stored only in this browser.

    Checks use the desk's hard limits (thresholds.py): age 15 min to 72 h, liquidity ≥ $12,000, 24h volume ≥ $40,000, market cap $60k to $8M, ≥ 150 trades in 24h, no buy-only hour, top wallet ≤ 5% (Solana, pool and program accounts excluded), top 10 ≤ 60%, ≥ 80 holders, no honeypot flag, Solana mint and freeze authority revoked. A fact nobody reported is UNKNOWN and never counts as a pass. Jev's judgement runs on the desk, not on this page.

    Data: DexScreener (prices, liquidity, trades), GeckoTerminal (holders, honeypot flag), Solana RPC (authorities, holders). Read-only: no keys, no signatures, no transactions.
